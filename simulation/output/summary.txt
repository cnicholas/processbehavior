========================================================================
KEY FINDINGS
========================================================================

1. Range-based estimator unbiased at SNR=0: YES (max |bias| = 1.76%)
2. GLM residual estimator unbiased at SNR=0: YES (max |bias| = 2.20%)
3. Naive MR-mixed bias at SNR=0: +0.07% (should be ~0)
   Naive MR-mixed bias at SNR=2: +7.76% (should be >0, contaminated)
4. Range-based precision at 90% singletons: mean std = 1.170
   Range-based precision at 10% singletons: mean std = 0.391
   Degradation ratio: 3.0x

5. The degradation is a PRECISION problem, not a BIAS problem.
   As singleton proportion increases, fewer subgroups contribute
   to the range-based estimate, increasing its variability.
   The estimate remains unbiased throughout.
