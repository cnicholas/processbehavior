## What is covered

- **Charts** — center line and both natural process limits, for the primary
  chart and for each valid chart x residual pair at that design state. Where subgroup
  sizes differ, VAS prints "UNEQUAL" instead of a limit; there the limits of every
  subgroup are read from the limit lines VAS draws, to within 1.5 drawing units
  (about 0.01-0.1, by chart), and each line counts as one assertion.
- **Capability** — Pp, Ppk (upper and lower), Cp, Cpk, and the percentage of
  the distribution beyond each specification limit.
- **Loss function** — the Taguchi loss decomposition and its components.

## Not compared with VAS

These outputs have no VAS reference in this run. They are covered by unit tests, and some by
other references, but no VAS value is checked:

- **Residual values one by one.** Charts of R1-R6 are compared through their limits above;
  the values themselves are not compared.
- **Signals** (run rules and points beyond the limits).
- **Staged (phased) Xbar and S charts.** These are checked against Wheeler's cavity data and
  Minitab's per-stage limits in `tests/test_phased_xbar_s.py`.
- **Effects and interaction tables**, calibrations, capability for a window of time, and the
  design report.

## Recovering known answers

`validation/truth_report.py` asks a different question: how closely the methods, as
implemented, recover answers known by construction (pure noise, and data built from known
effects). It is a report, not a gate. CI runs it on every push and keeps its output as the
`truth-report` artifact.

## Values with no reference yet

- The limits of the two factor S charts (pages 25 and 27) in the known-effects run, whose
  subgroups have 1000 and 2000 values. Its VAS run of 6 October 2026 held α<sub>N</sub>
  (11-7 manual Eq 9-8) at 0.9995 from N = 343 up, where Minitab's GAMMA function overflows,
  so its limits follow N = 500 instead of the subgroup's own N. Dr. Bishop's reruns of the PM
  runs (13 October 2026) compute α<sub>N</sub> for every N, and every R6 S chart in them is
  checked and matches; the known-effects run waits for the same rerun.
